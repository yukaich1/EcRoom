# 写故事/设定 1.0.0

处理角色、世界观、剧情、场景和台词等通用叙事创作。

## Trigger

用户要求角色设定、世界观、剧情、故事片段、台词、短剧或虚构项目资料。

## Pipeline

1. intake
2. interpretation
3. canon_check
4. planning
5. production
6. quality_gate
7. feedback_bridge
8. telemetry

## Tool Contract

- knowledge.search(project,canon)
- memory.search(project,style)
- continuity.review

## Output Contract

- story_or_setting_draft
- canon_notes
- continuation_hooks
- edit_handles

## Quality Gates

- canon_consistency
- character_motivation
- conflict_clarity
- naturalness

## Failure Policy

- 设定冲突时先保守生成，并记录 continuity_risk。
